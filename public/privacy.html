BIC Songbook Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

BIC Songbook helps approved members view and manage a shared library of songs.

Information used

When you sign in with Google, Google and our authentication provider, Supabase, process your account identifier, email address, and basic profile information, such as your name and profile picture. We use your account identifier and email to sign you in and manage access. BIC Songbook does not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or contacts.

We store account records, membership permissions, and songs submitted to the shared library. Songs are available to approved members. The owner manages membership using account email addresses; members with editing permission can change shared songs.

Service providers and browser storage

The website is hosted by Netlify. Supabase provides authentication and database storage, and Google provides Google sign-in. These providers process information needed to operate their services, including technical request information. Your browser stores sign-in session information and caches application files to support the installed website experience.

Use and sharing

We use account information to provide sign-in, enforce permissions, and operate the songbook. We do not sell account information or use Google account information for advertising. Shared song content can be viewed by approved members.

Retention and deletion

Account records and membership permissions are retained while needed to operate your access, unless you request deletion. To request access removal or deletion of your account data, contact the owner at the email below. Shared songs may remain in the library after your account is deleted; please identify any submitted content you also want removed. Service-provider backups and logs may remain subject to their retention policies.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email agatheojetro@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
